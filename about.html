Our History

Founded in 1995, the Information Technology Department at TPGIT has grown from a small program with just three faculty members to one of the leading computer science departments in the region. Our alumni have gone on to work at top tech companies and pursue advanced degrees at prestigious institutions worldwide.

1995

Department established with Bachelor's program

2002

Master's program introduced

2010

New computer science building opened

2020

Launched AI and Data Science specializations

Our Mission

To provide exceptional education in computer science through innovative teaching, cutting-edge research, and meaningful engagement with industry and community partners.
